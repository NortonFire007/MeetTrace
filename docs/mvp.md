# MeetTrace MVP at a glance

## User journey

1. Start MeetTrace.
2. Join Google Meet normally; no bot joins the call.
3. MeetTrace shows a compact floating control bar.
4. Start recording/transcription.
5. Speak naturally in English, Russian, Ukrainian, or a mix.
6. Pause/resume or stop from the floating bar.
7. MeetTrace writes `meeting.md` and `transcript.json` to the dated archive.
8. Open the meeting in the app and optionally generate a Gemini summary.

## MVP boundary

The MVP intentionally does not include speaker-name recognition, semantic search, task integrations, or video recording.
